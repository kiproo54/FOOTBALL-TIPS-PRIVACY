Privacy Policy

Application: Football predictions Effective Date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Football predictions ("we", "our", or "the App") handles, collects, uses, and safeguards user data and device information when you access or interact with our mobile application distributed via the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

We respect user privacy and adhere to data minimization principles. You are not required to create a user account, provide personal identity documents, or submit sensitive personally identifiable information (PII) to access football predictions.

  • Anonymous Diagnostic & Device Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic statistics (operating system version, device model, crash logs) generated automatically via Google Play services to ensure app stability and performance.
  • Subscription Tokens & Status: When you purchase a subscription tier, Google Play processes the transaction securely and issues an encrypted purchase token. Our billing client validates this token to grant access. We never receive, process, or store credit card, debit card, or financial banking details.

2. Google Play Advertising ID Declaration

Google Play Advertising ID Policy Compliance

Does this app use Advertising ID? NO

Football predictions does not collect, track, or utilize the Google Advertising ID for user profiling, behavioral analytics, or personalized targeted advertising.

The permission declaration com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is present in the merged manifest solely to ensure full compatibility with Google Play target API level requirements (Android 13 / API 33+) for standard bundled Google Play dependencies.

3. Subscriptions & Payment Processing

All in-app subscription options (including weekly and monthly passes) are handled exclusively via the official Google Play In-App Billing Library (v9+). Google Play is the merchant of record and manages all transactions, billing cycles, currency conversions, local tax collection, and auto-renewals.

Subscriptions can be cancelled, modified, or restored at any time by navigating to your Google account under Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & Subscriptions.

4. Real-Time Content & Match Data Feed

Match predictions, kickoff schedules, odds analysis, and settled match results are delivered dynamically via a read-only Google Firebase Realtime Database feed. Your query requests do not track your physical location or personal search behavior.

5. Age Requirement (18+)

Football predictions is exclusively intended for adult users aged 18 years or older. The application is not directed toward children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under any circumstances.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

The application relies on trusted infrastructure services that adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core application distribution, security verification, and subscription fulfillment.
  • Google Firebase: Cloud infrastructure for secure, real-time match predictions delivery.

7. Data Security & Retention

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols. Purchase entitlement tokens are stored locally on your device in secure sandbox storage so that you can access your purchased content without interruptions.
